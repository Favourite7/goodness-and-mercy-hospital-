How to Book an Appointment

Booking an appointment at Goodness and Mercy Hospital is simple and convenient. You can use our online form below, call us directly, or visit our hospital in person. Our team will confirm your appointment and send you a reminder. For emergency cases, please call our emergency hotline immediately or visit the hospital directly — no appointment needed for emergencies.

Important Information for Patients

⏰

Arrive Early

Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to complete registration and any necessary paperwork.

📋

Bring Documents

Bring a valid ID, any previous medical records, test results, and a list of current medications you are taking.

💰

Payment Options

We accept cash, bank transfers, and major health insurance plans. Please inquire about insurance coverage when you arrive.

🚫

Cancellation Policy

If you need to cancel or reschedule, please call us at least 24 hours in advance so we can offer the slot to another patient.

Need Immediate Medical Attention?

For emergencies, please do not wait for an appointment. Call our emergency hotline or visit the hospital immediately.
