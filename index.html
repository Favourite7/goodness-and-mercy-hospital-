Comprehensive Medical Services

At Goodness and Mercy Hospital, we offer a wide range of healthcare services designed to meet the diverse medical needs of our community. Our state-of-the-art facilities and experienced medical professionals ensure that every patient receives the highest standard of care.

🩺

General Medicine

Our general medicine department provides comprehensive health assessments, diagnosis, and treatment for a wide range of medical conditions. From routine checkups to chronic disease management, our experienced physicians are dedicated to maintaining and improving your overall health and well-being.

🤰

Maternity & Antenatal Care

We provide complete maternity services including antenatal care, safe delivery options, postnatal support, and neonatal care. Our maternity unit is equipped with modern facilities and staffed by compassionate professionals who prioritize the health and safety of both mother and child.

🧪

Laboratory & Diagnostics

Our fully equipped medical laboratory offers accurate and timely diagnostic testing including blood tests, urine analysis, microbiology, histopathology, and specialized investigations. We use modern equipment to ensure precise results that aid in effective treatment planning.

💊

Pharmacy Services

Our in-house pharmacy stocks a comprehensive range of quality medications, vaccines, and medical supplies. Our qualified pharmacists provide professional counseling on drug usage, dosage, and potential side effects to ensure safe and effective treatment outcomes.

🚑

Emergency & Critical Care

Our emergency department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, providing immediate medical attention for critical conditions, accidents, and urgent health concerns. Our rapid response team is always ready to deliver life-saving care when every second counts.

👶

Paediatrics & Child Health

Our paediatric department specializes in the health and medical care of infants, children, and adolescents. From immunizations and growth monitoring to treatment of childhood illnesses, we create a child-friendly environment that puts young patients and their parents at ease.

About Goodness and Mercy Hospital

Established in 2002, Goodness and Mercy Hospital has grown to become one of the most trusted healthcare institutions in the Soka area of Ibadan, Oyo State, Nigeria. For over two decades, we have been at the forefront of providing accessible, affordable, and high-quality medical care to individuals and families across the region.

Our hospital was founded with a clear mission: to deliver compassionate healthcare that treats every patient with dignity, respect, and personalized attention. We believe that quality medical services should be available to everyone, regardless of background or economic status.

Located at Lane L, House Number 9, in the serene Ajawele Area of Soka, Ibadan, our facility combines a peaceful healing environment with modern medical infrastructure. We continuously invest in upgrading our equipment and training our staff to ensure we meet international healthcare standards.
