Our Medical Team

Experienced, Compassionate, and Dedicated Healthcare Professionals

Meet the People Behind Your Care

At Goodness and Mercy Hospital, our strength lies in our people. Our medical team comprises over 50 qualified professionals including doctors, nurses, midwives, laboratory scientists, pharmacists, and support staff. Each member of our team brings unique expertise, genuine compassion, and an unwavering commitment to patient wellbeing. Together, we work tirelessly to ensure that every patient receives personalized, high-quality care in a respectful and comforting environment.

Hospital Leadership

Dr. Adesoji Mayokun Taiwo

Founder, Medical Director & Administrator

With over 30 years of experience in general medicine and healthcare administration, Dr. Taiwo founded Goodness and Mercy Hospital in 2002 with a vision to transform healthcare delivery in Ibadan. She holds advanced qualifications in family medicine and hospital management. Under her leadership, the hospital has grown from a small clinic to a 30-bed comprehensive medical facility serving thousands of patients annually. She is deeply committed to community health and continues to personally oversee critical cases and mentor young medical professionals.

MBBS Fellow, West African College of Physicians 30+ Years Experience

Adesoji Olamide Oluwaseun

Head of Nursing Services

A dedicated nursing leader with extensive experience in maternal health, emergency nursing, and patient care management. Adesoji Olamide Oluwaseun oversees our team of 25+ nurses and midwives, ensuring the highest standards of nursing excellence across all departments. She is passionate about mentoring young nurses, implementing best practices in patient care, and creating a supportive environment for both staff and patients. Her expertise in midwifery has contributed to the safe delivery of thousands of babies at our hospital.

Registered Nurse & Midwife BSc Nursing 20+ Years Experience
