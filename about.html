About Goodness and Mercy Hospital

Two Decades of Compassionate Healthcare Excellence in Ibadan

Our Story

Goodness and Mercy Hospital was founded in 2002 by Adesoji Mayokun Taiwo, a compassionate healthcare visionary who witnessed firsthand the struggles of families in the Ajawele community of Soka, Ibadan, seeking quality medical care. Growing up in the neighborhood, she saw neighbors travel long distances for basic treatments, pregnant women facing complications without proper care, and children suffering from preventable illnesses simply because there was no reliable hospital nearby.

Driven by a deep calling to serve humanity and guided by her faith, Adesoji Mayokun Taiwo made a bold decision to change this reality. With limited resources but unlimited determination, she established Goodness and Mercy Hospital at Lane L, House No 9, Ajawele Area, Soka. The name was inspired by Psalm 23:6 — "Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life" — reflecting her belief that every patient who enters the hospital deserves to experience divine goodness and mercy in their healing journey.

What started as a small clinic with just two consultation rooms and a handful of dedicated staff has blossomed into a 30-bed comprehensive medical facility serving thousands of patients annually. For 24 years, the hospital has remained a beacon of hope, delivering babies, saving lives in emergencies, diagnosing diseases with precision, and providing affordable medications to the community.

Today, under the continued leadership of Founder and Medical Director Adesoji Mayokun Taiwo, Goodness and Mercy Hospital stands as a testament to what vision, hard work, and genuine love for people can achieve. The hospital has become more than a medical facility — it is a trusted family member to every household in Soka and beyond.

Our Journey Through the Years

2002

Foundation

Goodness and Mercy Hospital was established by Adesoji Mayokun Taiwo in Soka, Ibadan, beginning with outpatient consultations and basic medical services in a modest facility.

2005

Maternity Services Launched

Introduced comprehensive maternity and antenatal care, becoming a trusted destination for expectant mothers in the community. First baby delivered safely at the hospital.

2008

Laboratory Expansion

Upgraded our diagnostic capabilities with modern laboratory equipment, enabling accurate and timely test results for better treatment outcomes.

2012

24/7 Emergency Services

Launched round-the-clock emergency and critical care unit. Expanded to 30 beds to accommodate more inpatients requiring continuous medical attention.

2015

Paediatrics Department

Established a dedicated paediatric unit with child-friendly facilities and specialized care for infants and children, including immunization programs.

2018

Community Health Programs

Launched free medical outreaches, health education seminars, and mobile clinic services to reach underserved communities around Ibadan.

2020

COVID-19 Response & Awards

Played a vital role in community health during the pandemic. Received recognition certificates from local health authorities for outstanding service.

2023

Strategic Partnerships

Formed partnerships with leading hospitals and healthcare organizations to enhance service delivery and provide specialist referrals.

2026

Digital Transformation

Launched our official website to improve patient access to information, online appointment booking, and healthcare resources.

Our Community Impact

Beyond the walls of our hospital, we are deeply committed to improving the health of our entire community through outreach programs and health education.

🚐

Free Medical Outreaches

Every quarter, our mobile clinic team visits rural communities around Ibadan providing free health screenings, consultations, and medications to those who cannot access hospital care.

📚

Health Education Programs

We conduct regular health talks at schools, churches, and community centers covering topics like malaria prevention, maternal health, nutrition, and hygiene practices.

🤝

Partnerships

We collaborate with leading hospitals in Ibadan and Lagos for specialist referrals, advanced diagnostics, and continuing medical education for our staff.

🏠

Home Visit Care

Our unique home visit service brings medical care to bedridden patients, elderly individuals, and post-surgical recovery cases who cannot travel to the hospital.
