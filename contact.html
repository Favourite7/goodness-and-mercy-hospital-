Find Us on the Map

We are conveniently located in the Soka area of Ibadan, easily accessible from major roads and landmarks. Use the map below to find directions to our hospital.

📍 Directions from Major Landmarks:

  • From Ibadan City Center (Dugbe): Head southwest toward Soka via Ring Road. Turn right at Soka junction, then left into Ajawele Area. Look for Lane L, House No 9.
  • From Ibadan Airport (IATA: IBA): Take the airport road toward the city, connect to Ring Road, and follow signs to Soka. Estimated travel time: 25-30 minutes.
  • From Lagos-Ibadan Expressway: Exit at Ibadan toll gate, follow the expressway into the city, then take Ring Road toward Soka. Estimated travel time from Lagos: 1.5 - 2 hours.
  • Public Transport: Ask any taxi or bus driver heading to "Soka" or "Ajawele Area." Most local drivers know the hospital by name.
