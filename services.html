Our Medical Services

Comprehensive Healthcare Solutions for Every Stage of Life

Healthcare Tailored to Your Needs

At Goodness and Mercy Hospital, we offer a full spectrum of medical services designed to meet the diverse healthcare needs of individuals, families, and the entire community. From preventive care to emergency treatment, our experienced medical team is equipped to provide the highest standard of care using modern technology and evidence-based practices.

🩺

General Medicine

Our General Medicine department serves as the foundation of healthcare at Goodness and Mercy Hospital. We provide comprehensive medical care for adults of all ages, addressing both acute illnesses and chronic conditions with expertise and compassion.

Our team of experienced physicians conducts thorough health assessments, accurate diagnoses, and personalized treatment plans. Whether you are experiencing fever, infections, hypertension, diabetes, or any other medical concern, we are here to help you regain and maintain optimal health.

What We Offer:

  • Comprehensive health checkups and wellness screenings
  • Management of chronic diseases (diabetes, hypertension, asthma)
  • Treatment of acute illnesses and infections
  • Preventive medicine and lifestyle counseling
  • Elderly care and geriatric medicine
  • Referrals to specialist care when needed
🤰

Maternity & Antenatal Care

Our Maternity Department is dedicated to ensuring the health and safety of mothers and their babies throughout pregnancy, childbirth, and the postnatal period. With over two decades of experience in maternal healthcare, we have safely delivered thousands of babies and supported countless families on their journey to parenthood.

We understand that pregnancy is one of the most significant experiences in a woman's life. Our team of skilled obstetricians, midwives, and nurses provides personalized care in a warm, supportive environment that prioritizes both medical excellence and emotional wellbeing.

What We Offer:

  • Pre-conception counseling and family planning
  • Comprehensive antenatal care and monitoring
  • Safe delivery services (normal and assisted)
  • Postnatal care and breastfeeding support
  • Neonatal care for newborns
  • Immunization programs for infants
  • Counseling on maternal nutrition and wellness
🧪

Laboratory & Diagnostics

Accurate diagnosis is the cornerstone of effective treatment. Our modern medical laboratory is equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic equipment and staffed by qualified laboratory scientists who ensure precise, reliable, and timely test results.

We perform a wide range of diagnostic tests that aid in the detection, monitoring, and management of diseases. Our laboratory maintains strict quality control measures and follows international standards to guarantee the accuracy of every result we deliver.

What We Offer:

  • Blood tests (full blood count, blood sugar, lipid profile, etc.)
  • Urine and stool analysis
  • Microbiology and culture tests
  • Pregnancy tests and hormone assays
  • Histopathology and cytology
  • Rapid diagnostic tests for malaria, typhoid, HIV, hepatitis
  • Health screening packages for individuals and organizations
💊

Pharmacy Services

Our in-house pharmacy ensures that patients have immediate access to the medications they need. Stocked with a comprehensive range of quality drugs, vaccines, and medical supplies, our pharmacy operates under the supervision of licensed pharmacists who prioritize patient safety and proper medication use.

We understand that taking medication correctly is essential for recovery. Our pharmacists take the time to counsel patients on dosage, timing, potential side effects, and drug interactions, ensuring that every prescription is used safely and effectively.

What We Offer:

  • Prescription dispensing and medication counseling
  • Over-the-counter medications and health supplements
  • Vaccines and immunization supplies
  • Medical equipment and consumables
  • Drug interaction checking and safety monitoring
  • Special orders for rare or specialized medications
🚑

Emergency & Critical Care

Medical emergencies require immediate, expert attention. Our Emergency Department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, providing rapid response and life-saving care for critically ill and injured patients. When every second counts, you can count on Goodness and Mercy Hospital.

Our emergency team comprises trained physicians, nurses, and support staff who are skilled in handling trauma, cardiac emergencies, respiratory distress, severe infections, accidents, and other urgent medical conditions. We are equipped with emergency medications, resuscitation equipment, and an ambulance for patient transport.

What We Offer:

  • 24/7 emergency medical treatment
  • Trauma and accident care
  • Cardiac emergency management
  • Respiratory and asthma emergency care
  • Paediatric emergencies
  • Ambulance services for patient transport
  • Stabilization and referral for advanced cases
👶

Paediatrics & Child Health

Children are not just small adults — they require specialized medical care that addresses their unique developmental, emotional, and physical needs. Our Paediatrics Department is dedicated to providing comprehensive healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents in a friendly, comforting environment.

From routine wellness checks and immunizations to the treatment of childhood illnesses and developmental assessments, our paediatric team ensures that every child receives gentle, thorough, and age-appropriate care. We work closely with parents to promote healthy growth and development.

What We Offer:

  • Well-child visits and growth monitoring
  • Immunization and vaccination programs
  • Treatment of common childhood illnesses
  • Management of childhood asthma and allergies
  • Nutritional counseling for children
  • Developmental assessments
  • Neonatal care for newborns

Additional Specialized Services

Beyond our core departments, Goodness and Mercy Hospital offers specialized services that address specific healthcare needs and enhance patient convenience.

🏠

Home Visit Care

Our unique home visit service brings qualified medical professionals to patients who are unable to visit the hospital due to illness, age, or mobility challenges. We provide home consultations, nursing care, medication administration, and post-surgical follow-up in the comfort of your home.

🚐

Mobile Clinic

Through our mobile clinic program, we extend healthcare services to remote communities, schools, churches, and workplaces around Ibadan. This service includes health screenings, consultations, immunizations, and health education.

🩻

Imaging & Ultrasound

Our diagnostic imaging services include ultrasound scanning for pregnancy monitoring, abdominal assessments, and other soft tissue examinations. We use modern ultrasound equipment operated by trained sonographers.

🦷

Dental Care

Basic dental services including tooth extraction, cleaning, and oral health education are available at our facility. We emphasize preventive dental care and proper oral hygiene practices.

👁️

Eye Care

Our eye care services include vision testing, treatment of common eye conditions, and referrals for specialized ophthalmological care. We promote regular eye checkups for all age groups.

🧠

Mental Health Support

We recognize the importance of mental health in overall wellbeing. Our team provides counseling, stress management guidance, and support for patients dealing with anxiety, depression, and other mental health concerns.

How to Access Our Services

1

Walk In or Book

Visit our hospital directly or book an appointment online or by phone. Our reception is open 24/7.

2

Registration

New patients complete a simple registration form. Returning patients proceed directly to consultation.

3

Consultation

See a qualified doctor who will assess your condition, recommend tests if needed, and prescribe treatment.

4

Treatment

Receive prescribed medications, undergo procedures, or get admitted for inpatient care as recommended.

5

Follow-Up

Return for follow-up visits as advised. Our staff will remind you of upcoming appointments.
